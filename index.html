＋Other incomeBenefits, freelance work, rent and moreOptional

Enter annual taxable amounts only. For business and rent, use your net taxable profit after allowable expenses; do not enter gross receipts. Loss carry-forwards and cross-source loss offsets need a separate computation.

−Expenses & donationsAllowable costs and qualifying giftsOptional

Employment expenses must be wholly and exclusively incurred to earn your income and not reimbursed. Enter only qualifying donations to approved IPCs or the Singapore Government; the calculator applies the 2.5× deduction. Check donation eligibility ↗

◇Retirement savingsSRS contributions and CPF cash top-upsOptional

Enter eligible amounts contributed in the income year. CPF top-ups must meet recipient balance and family eligibility rules. Exclude transfers and amounts attracting matching grants. SRS withdrawals in the same year can reduce relief. Review the rules.

♡Family & other reliefsDependants, National Service and other claimsOptional
Enter your eligible share of each relief, after checking individual limits. These fields record your claim; they do not determine eligibility. Avoid including the same relief twice.

Child reliefs include QCR, disability child relief and WMCR. Combined limits and birth/adoption dates can affect your claim. Parent/caregiver reliefs may be shared with family. Other reliefs can include eligible self-employed CPF/MediSave, life insurance or sibling disability relief. Course fees relief has lapsed from YA 2026. Check all eligibility and limits with IRAS ↗

✓Tax rebatesAvailable Parenthood Tax Rebate balanceOptional

Personal income tax guide
1

Choose the right year

Your Year of Assessment (YA) usually covers income earned in the previous calendar year. YA 2026 covers income from 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2025.

YA 2027 in this tool is a planning projection for 2026 income, using published rules as checked on 15 September 2026.

2

Check tax residency

Tax residency determines which rules apply. A foreigner can be a Singapore tax resident; nationality alone does not decide it. Duration-of-stay rules and concessions may apply.

This estimator calculates resident tax only. Non-resident employment, director fees, short-term work and treaty exemptions need their own treatment.

Check residency and rates at IRAS ↗
3

Build up your taxable income

Include salary, bonus and taxable benefits. Add net taxable trade or freelance income and your share of net rental income. Enter only taxable other income; not every receipt, investment gain or overseas payment is taxable.

Deduct allowable employment expenses and qualifying donations. Then personal reliefs reduce income to your chargeable income.

Check what is taxable ↗
4

Check reliefs before claiming

Reliefs reduce taxable income; rebates reduce tax itself. Most reliefs have eligibility, sharing and individual limits, as well as the overall $80,000 personal relief cap.

Detailed fields accept your eligible allocated amount. For family reliefs, agree the allocation and check combined limits so the same relief is not claimed twice.
